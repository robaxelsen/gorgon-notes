How fights get here: each player's own Gorgon Notes sends a fight when it ends (and the ones fought while it was closed, at its next start), with or without a party code. Nothing of yours here? Start Gorgon Notes with chat logging on (Settings → V.I.P. → Save Chat Logs); the Party window shows what was sent.

How they are grouped: fights whose times overlap are one fight, with one row per player; when both chat logs name a hunting party and neither names the other player, they stay apart. Fights in one area with no gap over 15 minutes are one run. Strangers fighting in the same area at the same time can land in one run: saying "I was not in this group" is the next step.

Each row counts what that player's own log saw: their hits and their pets', with damage over time after their first hit. Per second divides by the seconds of fighting that attacker was in. Times are shown in your clock.
